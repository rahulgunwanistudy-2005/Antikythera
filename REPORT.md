## Primary result

Current status: **pending_scientific_validation**.
No audited headline effect size is released. Computed exploratory outputs, if eligible data exists, remain separated from audited results. The curated legends never become empirical outcomes.

Among species/indication pairs first tested inside the fully observed 20 years after each freeze, the analysis compares earlier claims against tested unclaimed controls. Confirmation needs an original, reviewed botanical in-vivo or clinical supporting study. Missing records remain unknown. Evidence for a purified compound does not automatically confirm a botanical preparation.

Three matched comparisons use the same species, the same family/indication, or similar pre-freeze research effort. Publication volume is a ranking baseline. Identity sampling and family bootstrap propagate uncertainty. A separate sensitivity resamples families and refits matching. Era, category and novel/inherited layers are computed independently.

The 2010 primary window matures in 2030. A separately named available-followup sensitivity ends at the declared observation year. It must not be compared as if it were another complete 20-year window.

## Limitations

- Independent human audits, historical-date/identity review and complete evidence coverage remain release gates.
- Model probabilities condition on future first testing and are not clinical efficacy or the chance of a future study.
- Claim dates and identity prior availability must both precede a temporal freeze.
- Whole-plant preparations and compound-only evidence are kept distinct.
- Complete 20-year follow-up for freeze 2010 ends in 2030; shorter follow-up is a labelled sensitivity.
- PubMed/EuropePMC coverage is insufficient to establish exhaustive pre-1900 pharmacology.
- Publication-bias grids assume hypothetical unseen negative pairs; they do not estimate actual missing-study rates.
- Identity/family fit-ensemble intervals are not confidence intervals for an established medicinal effect.
