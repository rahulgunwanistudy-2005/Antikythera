# Antikythera interface

An interactive research instrument with the feel of a botanical collection, rather than a marketing page.

Palette: field #eff3ed; paper #ffffff; ink #173f39; leaf #688776; brass #af8746; caution #9b4944. Georgia carries the historical question and the year; the system sans serif keeps controls and records legible. Data stays left aligned. A restrained circular mechanism is the single expressive element, connecting the time control to the project's name. The plant matrix is the primary workspace; a specimen pane keeps the selected claim and its provenance adjacent. Mobile collapses navigation and stacks the detail pane beneath the matrix.

Review against the brief: no invented confirmation rate, no decorated confidence intervals, no candidate probabilities without calibrated identities. Discovery milestones have their own visual state. Audit status remains visible. Every tab provides a useful view, and exports include provenance and pending-result status. Illustrations are schematic botanical line art, never identification aids.
