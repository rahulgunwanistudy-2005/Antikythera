# Curated demonstration catalog

`catalog.json` is a six-case reading guide, not a sample from the analytical population. It must never supply denominators, risk ratios, precision@k, identity probabilities, first-tested years or forward predictions. `analysis_eligible` is false. All claims are `curated_unreviewed`, no primary spans have been extracted, all candidate probabilities are null, and displayed years can be approximate anchors. Two cases (Pacific yew and Madagascar periwinkle) are external comparisons rather than Mediterranean cohort members. Cancer milestones remain outside the twelve organ-system categories.

Source checks on 7 November 2026:

- Ursin & Steger's abstract, PMID 30557131, supports ancient podagra use already in the second century and fifth-century Jacobus recipes. It does not establish a measured Byzantine-era ranking lift.
- ANSES supports 28 autumn-crocus confusions with wild **or many-flowered** garlic in France, 2020–22, including two deaths.
- McGill's narrative supports Paskov's work in the 1950s. It is a secondary narrative; exact 1956 isolation and first written Balkan use remain unresolved. Its confident moly identification is treated as contested.
- Bailey's 2017 historical overview supports the 1918 guanidine and 1957 metformin milestones. This is not evidence for a whole-plant diabetes preparation.
- DEA Museum dates morphine isolation to 1805. Other authoritative accounts date it differently; the brief's 1804 is not silently adopted.
- NCI documents Pacific yew bark collection in 1962, and mouse-model development in 1977. Pacific yew is **Taxus brevifolia**, not Mediterranean **T. baccata**.
- Noble's discovery-review search excerpt (PMID 2085431) connects reputed antidiabetic use to vinca-alkaloid discovery. Full abstract retrieval was partially blocked; precise isolation years remain pending.

These are paraphrases with source links, not copied full articles. Narrative access does not clear full-text redistribution rights. Seed content is original project summarisation; source material retains its original terms. Exact isolation dates, historical first-written dates, source-language spans, species identities and licences require additional review before slide claims or analysis.
